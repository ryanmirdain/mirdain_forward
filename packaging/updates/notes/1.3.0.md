Forward now opens its own window when it starts. The first time, it walks you through setup: choose whether Forward launches at sign-in and sends notifications, sign in to Mirdain in your browser, and pick your organization. Forward then lists that organization's printers in the menu bar and offers to add one, with the same printer setup as mirdainsystems.com/forward.

Afterwards, Forward opens straight to the slicer, kept on your organization. Use **Add Printer…** and **Organization** in the menu to add printers or switch organizations, and **Preferences…** to change your startup, notification, and update choices.
