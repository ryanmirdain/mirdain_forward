Send models to Gwaith straight from your desktop and it slices them in the background. On macOS, drag an STL or 3MF onto a printer in the panel that appears under Forward's menu bar icon, and add optional notes for Gwaith. On Windows, right-click a model in Explorer and choose **Send to Gwaith**, then the printer. When Gwaith finishes, Forward sends the plate to that printer. Click a run to open its session and chat in the slicer.

Forward's notifications now show progress as plates download, send and slice, and update in place. On Windows they appear in Forward's own card by the tray icon instead of system notifications.

Bambu printers are more reliable to reach: Forward retries when a sleeping printer's Wi-Fi is slow to wake, and connection errors name the address it tried. On Windows, setup and the installer have a refreshed look, closing Forward's window no longer leaves it blank when reopened, and the window uses less memory while hidden.
